Filipino Spaghetti

A sweet-style pasta dish commonly served at Filipino celebrations.

Description

Filipino spaghetti is known for its slightly sweet tomato-based sauce, ground meat, and sliced hotdogs. It is often served during birthdays, family gatherings, and other celebrations.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
15 minutes 30 minutes 4-5 people Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 g spaghetti noodles
  • 250 g ground pork or beef
  • 3–4 hotdogs, sliced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato or Filipino-style spaghetti sauce
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Grated cheese, for topping

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan. Sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  3. Add the ground meat and cook until browned.
  4. Add the sliced hotdogs and cook for a few minutes.
  5. Pour in the spaghetti sauce. Stir and simmer for about 10–15 minutes.
  6. Season with salt and pepper. Add a little water if the sauce is too thick.
  7. Pour the sauce over the cooked noodles and finish with grated cheese.
