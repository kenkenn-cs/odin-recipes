About this dish

Chicken adobo is simmered until tender in a savory, tangy mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, pepper, and bay leaves.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
    • \n
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
    • \n
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
    • \n
  • 1 cup water
    • \n
  • 1 head garlic, lightly crushed
    • \n
  • 2 bay leaves
    • \n
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
    • \n
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil (optional)
    • \n

Instructions

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and half of the garlic. Marinate for 20–30 minutes if time allows.
    2. \n
  2. Heat the oil in a pot. Brown the chicken lightly in batches, if desired.
    3. \n
  3. Add the marinade, vinegar, water, remaining garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns. Bring to a boil without stirring for the first few minutes after adding the vinegar.
    4. \n
  4. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for about 30–40 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
    5. \n
  5. Taste the sauce. Simmer uncovered to reduce it if you prefer a stronger flavor. Serve hot with rice.
    6. \n

Cooking notes

Cooking time depends on the size of the chicken pieces. Make sure poultry is thoroughly cooked.
