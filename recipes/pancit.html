About this dish

Pancit canton combines wheat noodles, vegetables, and a savory sauce. It is useful for a quick shared meal.

Ingredients

  • 250 g pancit canton noodles
    • \n
  • 200 g chicken breast or other protein, thinly sliced
    • \n
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
    • \n
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
    • \n
  • 1 small onion, sliced
    • \n
  • 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
    • \n
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
    • \n
  • 1/2 cup green beans, sliced
    • \n
  • 2 cups chicken stock or water (adjust to noodle package directions)
    • \n
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
    • \n
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce (optional)
    • \n
  • Black pepper to taste
    • \n

Instructions

  1. Prepare and slice all ingredients before heating the pan. Check the noodle package for its recommended liquid amount and cooking time.
    2. \n
  2. Heat the oil in a large pan. Cook the chicken until no longer pink and fully cooked. Transfer to a plate if needed.
    3. \n
  3. Sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant. Add the carrot, green beans, and cabbage; stir-fry briefly.
    4. \n
  4. Pour in the stock, soy sauce, and optional oyster sauce. Bring the liquid to a gentle boil.
    5. \n
  5. Add the noodles and toss as they soften, following the package directions. Return the cooked chicken to the pan.
    6. \n
  6. Continue tossing until the noodles absorb most of the liquid and are tender. Season with pepper and serve.
    7. \n

Cooking notes

Noodle brands absorb different amounts of liquid. Add stock gradually if needed, and avoid overcooking the vegetables.
