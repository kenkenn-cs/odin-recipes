Mango Float

A chilled no-bake Filipino dessert made with mangoes, cream, and graham crackers.

Description

Mango float is a simple refrigerator cake that does not require baking. Layers of graham crackers, sweetened cream, and ripe mangoes soften and set as the dessert chills.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
20 minutes No cooking required 6-8 people Easy

Ingredients

  • 2–3 ripe mangoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 2 cups all-purpose cream, chilled
  • ½ cup condensed milk, or to taste
  • 1–2 packs graham crackers
  • Crushed graham crackers, for topping

Instructions

  1. Combine the chilled all-purpose cream and condensed milk in a bowl. Mix until smooth.
  2. Arrange a layer of graham crackers at the bottom of a rectangular dish. Break crackers as needed to fill gaps.
  3. Spread a layer of the cream mixture over the crackers.
  4. Add a layer of sliced mangoes.
  5. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, finishing with cream and mango on top.
  6. Sprinkle crushed graham crackers over the dessert.
  7. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight before serving.
