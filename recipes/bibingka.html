Bibingka

A soft and fluffy Filipino rice cake traditionally enjoyed during the Christmas season.

Description

Bibingka is a traditional Filipino rice cake made from rice flour, coconut milk, and sugar. It is commonly baked in a clay pot lined with banana leaves and topped with salted egg, cheese, butter, and grated coconut. It is especially popular during the Christmas season.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
15 minutes 25-30 minutes 6 people Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice flour
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 2 salted eggs, sliced
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • ½ cup grated coconut, for topping
  • Banana leaves, for lining (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Prepare a round baking pan and line it with banana leaves or lightly grease it.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine the rice flour, all-purpose flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, whisk together the eggs, coconut milk, milk, and melted butter.
  4. Gradually pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Mix until the batter becomes smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan.
  6. Arrange the sliced salted eggs and grated cheese on top of the batter.
  7. Bake for about 25–30 minutes or until the center is set and the top becomes lightly golden.
  8. Remove from the oven and brush with butter. Add grated coconut before serving.
