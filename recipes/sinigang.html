About this dish

Pork sinigang is a comforting soup known for its pleasantly sour broth and tender vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 750 g pork belly or shoulder, cut into pieces
    • \n
  • 8 cups water
    • \n
  • 1 onion, quartered
    • \n
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
    • \n
  • 1 packet tamarind soup base (use according to package directions)
    • \n
  • 1 radish, sliced
    • \n
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
    • \n
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong), trimmed
    • \n
  • 2 green chilies (optional)
    • \n
  • Fish sauce, to taste
    • \n

Instructions

  1. Place the pork and water in a pot. Bring to a boil and skim off any foam that rises to the surface.
    2. \n
  2. Add the onion and tomatoes. Lower the heat and simmer until the pork is becoming tender, about 45–60 minutes.
    3. \n
  3. Add the tamarind soup base according to its package directions. Taste the broth before adding more souring mix.
    4. \n
  4. Add the radish and eggplant. Simmer until the vegetables are tender but not falling apart.
    5. \n
  5. Add the water spinach and chilies. Cook briefly until the leaves wilt. Season with fish sauce and serve hot.
    6. \n

Cooking notes

Follow the instructions on your soup-base packet because brands differ in strength. Adjust sourness and seasoning to taste.
